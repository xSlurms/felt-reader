Felt Reader — Free Poker Decision Tool

Should you call or fold? Felt Reader is a free, browser-based poker tool that reads the board and does the math for you. Enter your two cards, the flop, turn, and river, and the bet you're facing — it shows your equity, warns you about flushes and straights, and gives you a clear call, fold, or raise. Built for beginners and low-stakes players learning when to fold, when to chase a draw, and when they're beat. No sign-up, works on your phone, free forever.
